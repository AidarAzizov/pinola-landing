Why Pinola

Not an algorithm.
Your people.

Pinola shows you where the people you trust have actually been. Built by a traveler — to share the places worth going with friends, and to keep the memory of a spot, the day, and the thoughts that came with it, pinned right on the map.

Only your people
No algorithm decides what you see — only the friends whose taste you actually trust. A recommendation from a real person beats a thousand reviews.
A memory, not just a pin
Every place keeps the photo, the day, and the thoughts you had standing there. Years later, open the pin and you're back in that moment.
Your life, as a map
Every country, every spot you've shared lights up on your profile. A living diary of everywhere you've been — and proof of how far you've gone.
Two modes

New posts.
This area.

Switch between a real-time feed of what's happening now and a full map of everything ever posted in any area you're exploring.

How it works

Simple by design

Three steps to building your travel map.

1
Post a place
Add a photo, a few words, and a location. It lands on the map for everyone who follows you.
2
Follow your people
Add friends whose travel taste you trust. Their posts appear on your map — nowhere else.
3
Plan your next trip
Save posts into collections. Share a collection with whoever you're traveling with. Open it on the map when you arrive.
